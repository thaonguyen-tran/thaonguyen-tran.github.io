Selected research projects

Bayesian Inference for Discrete Time Stochastic Volatility

Master’s thesis · Ca’ Foscari University of Venice · Nov 2025 – Jul 2026

Supervisor: Professor Stefano Federico Tonellato

Explored Bayesian state-space models for financial time series, stochastic volatility and latent dynamics using MCMC. Investigated probabilistic forecasting, multivariate factor models and posterior convergence diagnostics.

Bayesian Tangency Portfolio

Course-based research · Oct 2025 – Mar 2026

Studied portfolio optimisation under conjugate priors, parameter uncertainty, stochastic representations and simulation-based inference of portfolio weights.

Time-Series Analysis

Course-based research · Jun 2025

Investigated stationarity, dependence, conditional variance, residual diagnostics and short-horizon probabilistic forecasts.

The Effects of Merger and Acquisition Announcements on the Wealth of Bidding Shareholders in Short Term: An Empirical Evidence of Vietnam

Bachelor’s thesis · Dec 2019 – May 2020

Supervisor: Professor Tan Khoa Duong

Applied event-study methods and abnormal-return estimators to investigate announcement-driven returns and cross-sectional variation.
