About me

I am a PhD student in Statistical Sciences at the University of Bologna, Italy.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Finance and Banking from the University of Economics Ho Chi Minh City and a Master's degree in Quantitative Finance and Risk Management from Ca’ Foscari University of Venice. Prior to my master's studies, I gained over five years of professional experience across various roles in finance. I have also passed Level II of the CFA Program.

My academic journey has evolved from the application of quantitative models towards a deeper understanding and development of statistical methodologies. This transition motivated me to pursue a PhD in Statistical Sciences, with the aim of developing methodological approaches that can address complex research problems across diverse disciplines.
